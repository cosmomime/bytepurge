Privacy Policy

Storage Cleaner: Cache & Junk (com.cosmomime.bytepurge)
Developer: COSMOMIME (PT KOSMOS PERANTI NUSANTARA) • Last Updated: November 16, 2026

PT KOSMOS PERANTI NUSANTARA (operating under developer brand COSMOMIME) built Storage Cleaner: Cache & Junk (the “App”) as an on-device Android storage management utility with optional Pro features available via Google Play In-App Purchases and Subscriptions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses on your device, why specific Android permissions are requested, and how your data is protected.

100% On-Device Processing — Your files, photos, videos, filenames, and installed app lists are never uploaded, collected, or sold.

1. Information We Access (Strictly On-Device)

The App does not require user registration, accounts, emails, or logins. All scanning, duplicate detection, file inspection, and media compression happen locally on your Android device.

Stored Locally Only: Your clean history summary (Savings Log), protected folder whitelist, threshold settings, and Pro entitlement cache stay strictly inside private Android app storage on your device.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Storage Permissions): Required solely to scan device directories for orphaned uninstalled-app leftovers (e.g., inside Android/data and Android/obb), temporary caches, empty folders, large files, exact duplicates, visually similar photos, messenger media, downloaded files, APK installers, and to perform user-initiated deletions or photo/video compression. No file contents or paths leave your device.
  • Query Installed Apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Used locally to display installed user and system apps in App Manager, match residual folders against currently installed packages to avoid deleting active app data, and launch system uninstall/app info screens or extract user-selected APK backups. We do not collect or transmit your list of installed applications.
  • Package Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS, Optional): Used locally on Android to calculate accurate per-app code, data, and cache sizes in App Manager. Usage data is never logged remotely or shared.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Used to display real-time scan/cleanup progress notifications and optional Pro Storage Sentinel low-storage reminders.
  • Shizuku Integration (Optional User-Initiated Tool): If you install and authorize Shizuku on your device, the App uses Shizuku locally only when you explicitly trigger cache trimming in App Manager.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to communicate with Google Play Billing for In-App Purchases and Subscriptions, and to transmit anonymous crash reports and aggregated analytics diagnostics via Firebase.

3. In-App Purchases, Analytics & Advertising

A. Google Play Billing

Optional Pro upgrades (Pro Lifetime, Pro Annual, Pro Monthly) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or have access to your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address. Google Play provides non-personally-identifiable purchase tokens and subscription states solely to verify and maintain your Pro entitlement.

B. Firebase Crashlytics & Firebase Analytics

Both debug and release builds integrate Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to diagnose unexpected crashes and understand general feature usage (such as app version, Android OS version, device model, coarse country/region derived from IP address, anonymized app-instance and installation identifiers, crash stack traces, and screen interactions). Your personal files, photos, videos, filenames, and installed app lists are never sent to Firebase. Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS. Review Firebase privacy practices at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

C. Advertising

Version 1.0 of the App is ad-free, does not request advertisements, and does not access the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID). If future versions introduce advertising (such as Google AdMob), this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated prior to release.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

Except for payment processing handled by Google Play and diagnostic/analytics telemetry handled by Google Firebase as described above, the App does not share, sell, rent, or transfer personal or device data to any third parties.

Review Google’s privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because the App operates locally without developer servers or user accounts:

  • You can delete your local Savings Log inside the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android System Settings removes all locally saved preferences, whitelists, and logs.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data in connection with support requests, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or legal requirements. Changes take effect when posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.
